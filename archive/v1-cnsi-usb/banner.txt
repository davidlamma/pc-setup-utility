 *   How to use: The program will take you step-by-step. You will need to answer Y or N.          *

 *   Setup: Uninstalls current winget version installed and reinstalls an updated version.        *

 *                                                                                                * 
 *   Uninstall: Winget searches program names and uninstalls matching app IDs in the batch file.  * 
 *   Note: Watch for any uninstaller pop-ups that may appear. May have to run as admin.           *
